 240 H x 228 W

 8px at 208-215 H

 4px at 0-3 W
 4px at 224-227 W

Display:
 Scores at 180-193 H
  Player 1 Score at 20-63 W
  Player 2 Score at 148-191 W
  Digits are 12px by 14 px
 Status at 194-207 H
  Player # at 4-15 W
  Ball # at 132-143 W

Bricks:
 31px at 153-183 H
  14x8 array of bricks
    14px wide, 2px column gaps
    3px tall, 1px row gaps

Serve Line:
 103 H

Paddle:
 4px at 32-35 H
 16px or 8x wide

Ball:
 2px H x 4px W
 3 speeds both hori & vert
  1px, 2px, 3px / frame

  4th volley - 1px -> 2px
  8th volley - 2px -> 1px
  12th volley - 1px -> 2px
  Bricks (tiers 5-8) -> 3px
 Hori speed changes
  Center of paddle -> 1px
  Edges of paddle -> 2px
  8th volley -> 3px

Switches:
 3 or 5 balls per credit
 Upright vs Cocktail (screen flip)
 1 or 2 credits per coin
 Free Game (+1 credit) at 100/200/300/400/500/600/700/800 points

  ticks once per point scored, but can be overflowed/overloaded as it only holds up to 15 ticks
